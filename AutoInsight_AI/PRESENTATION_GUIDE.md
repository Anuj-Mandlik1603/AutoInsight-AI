## Common Viva Questions & Answers

### Q1: How is your project different from Tableau or Power BI?
**Answer**: "Traditional BI tools require manual configuration and technical expertise. AutoInsight AI automatically detects KPIs, generates visualizations, and most importantly, uses Generative AI to provide natural language insights and recommendations. A manager can get actionable business advice in minutes without knowing how to create charts."

### Q2: How exactly do you use Generative AI?
**Answer**: "We extract key metrics, trends, and anomalies from the data, structure them into a detailed prompt with business context, send it to Google Gemini API, and parse the natural language response into executive summaries, findings, and recommendations. The AI transforms numbers into business insights."

### Q3: What if the AI hallucinates or gives wrong insights?
**Answer**: "We ground the AI in actual data by providing specific numerical context in prompts. We also display 'AI-Generated' labels and show source data alongside insights, encouraging users to verify. Additionally, we structure prompts to request specific, data-backed responses only."

### Q4: Can this handle large datasets?
**Answer**: "Currently optimized for datasets up to 100MB. For larger datasets, we can implement sampling strategies, database integration, and distributed processing in future versions. The current implementation suits SMEs and academic use cases perfectly."

### Q5: What algorithms do you use for anomaly detection?
**Answer**: "We use the Interquartile Range (IQR) method for outlier detection - calculating Q1, Q3, and identifying values beyond 1.5 * IQR from the quartiles. We also use standard deviation-based methods and trend analysis for time-series anomalies."

### Q6: How do you ensure data privacy?
**Answer**: "All processing happens locally on the user's machine. We only send aggregated statistics and metrics to the AI API, not raw data. No data is stored on our servers. Users maintain complete ownership of their data."

### Q7: What makes this a good B.Tech project?
**Answer**: "It demonstrates full-stack development, data science, machine learning, AI integration, software engineering principles, and solves a real business problem. It covers multiple domains from the curriculum and showcases practical application of Generative AI."

### Q8: Can you monetize this?
**Answer**: "Yes. Freemium model: free tier with limited analyses, premium with unlimited reports, white-label solutions for enterprises, and API marketplace. Target market: 50,000+ SMEs in India alone."
